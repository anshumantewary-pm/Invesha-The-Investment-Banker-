# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Company valuation: projections, WACC, DCF (perpetuity and exit), comparables, LBO, scenarios, Monte Carlo, reverse DCF, credit profile and executive summary
- Project finance: sculpted or annuity debt, DSCR / LLCR, IRRs, break-even tariff and stress tests
- Startup model: monthly model, runway, unit economics, VC method and fundraising plan
- M&A accretion / dilution model
- Automatic input detection from uploaded statements, MIS reports and term sheets
- Live-formula Excel export for every model
- Web platform with accounts, My models, sample library and Invesha Academy
