# Startup model methodology

Monthly simulation: new customers grow at a monthly rate, churn removes a share of the opening base, MRR =
customers × ARPU (ARPU rises once a year), gross profit = MRR × gross margin, marketing = new customers × CAC,
team and overheads grow monthly, and cash moves by EBITDA plus funding received.

Outputs: runway (months until cash turns negative), break-even month (first month after which EBITDA stays
positive), funding gap, LTV = ARPU × gross margin ÷ churn, LTV/CAC, CAC payback, burn multiple (year-1 burn ÷
ARR added), Rule of 40, a VC-method valuation (exit ARR × multiple ÷ target return) and a suggested raise.
