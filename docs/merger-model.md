# M&A accretion / dilution methodology

- Offer price = target share price × (1 + premium); equity purchase price = offer × target shares; fees are paid in cash.
- Consideration: stock (new shares at the acquirer's price), new debt, and cash on hand (the remainder plus fees).
- Pro forma net income (years 1 and 2) = acquirer NI + target NI + after-tax synergies (phased in)
  − after-tax interest on new debt − after-tax forgone interest on cash − after-tax intangibles amortisation.
- Pro forma EPS = pro forma NI ÷ (acquirer shares + new shares); accretion = pro forma EPS ÷ standalone EPS − 1.
- Breakeven synergies: the pre-tax run-rate that makes year-1 EPS neutral.
- Also: offer vs acquirer P/E, ownership, contribution analysis, pro forma leverage and a premium × stock-mix grid.
