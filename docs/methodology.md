# Company valuation methodology

## Projections

Revenue grows at a rate that fades linearly from year-1 growth to final-year growth (or follows an explicit
per-year path). The EBITDA margin fades from today's level to the target margin. D&A and capex are a share
of revenue; the increase in net working capital is NWC % × the change in revenue.

## Free cash flow

FCFF = EBIT × (1 − tax) + D&A − capex − increase in NWC.

## Discount rate

Cost of equity from CAPM (risk-free rate + beta × equity risk premium + size premium). Beta is the sector's
unlevered beta relevered to the target debt weight unless given. WACC weights after-tax cost of debt and
cost of equity.

## Terminal value and enterprise value

Two methods: perpetuity growth, FCFF × (1 + g) ÷ (WACC − g), and exit multiple, final EBITDA × EV/EBITDA.
The headline enterprise value averages both. Equity value = EV − debt + cash.

## Cross-checks

Trading comparables (EV/EBITDA LTM and NTM, EV/Revenue, P/E), bear / base / bull scenarios, a seeded Monte
Carlo simulation, a reverse DCF, an LBO and a credit profile. Sector multiples and betas are indicative.
