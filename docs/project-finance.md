# Project finance methodology

1. Revenue = capacity × utilisation × price, escalating each year; opex = fixed + variable, escalating.
2. CFADS = EBITDA − tax (losses carried forward).
3. Debt = min(gearing × total cost, PV at the debt rate of CFADS ÷ target DSCR over the tenor).
   Interest during construction is capitalised; debt sizing and tax are iterated to convergence.
4. Repayment is sculpted (flat DSCR) or an annuity.
5. Outputs: DSCR by year, minimum and average DSCR, LLCR, project and equity IRR and NPV, payback, the
   break-even price for the equity hurdle, and stress tests (price, capex, opex, utilisation, rate, delay).
